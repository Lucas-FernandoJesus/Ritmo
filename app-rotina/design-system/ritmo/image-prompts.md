# Imagens de fundo do Ritmo

Modo: geração com a ferramenta interna de ImageGen; os arquivos escolhidos foram convertidos para WebP (qualidade 0,86) para uso no aplicativo. Não há dependência de imagens remotas.

## Textura de fundo

Destino: `src/assets/ebony-walnut-texture.webp`.

Prompt final:

> Create a seamless-feeling photographic background texture for a mobile-first personal planner app named Ritmo. Close-up straight-on view of dark ebony-stained walnut wood with a restrained warm varnish sheen and fine natural grain, charcoal-black overall with deep espresso and muted amber-brown grain lines. Sophisticated vintage material character, not rustic, not distressed, no objects, no lettering, no logos, no borders, no heavy highlights, no dramatic diagonal streaks. Even low-key lighting, subdued midtones so UI overlays remain legible. Intended as a subtle repeatable app background asset; material texture must be visible but quiet. Photorealistic, high-resolution square composition.

## Cabeçalho

Destino: `src/assets/lacquered-desk-header.webp`.

Prompt final:

> Photorealistic wide background photograph for the compact heading panel of a mobile personal planner app. A vintage black walnut writing desk seen from directly above: deep charcoal lacquered wood, subtle amber varnish highlights, elegant fine grain. A soft narrow shaft of warm afternoon light grazes only the far right side; the left and center stay dark and visually quiet for white UI text. Very restrained hint of an old brass desk edge at the extreme right border only, no other objects. Premium, intimate, calm, not rustic. Wide horizontal composition suitable for cropping on mobile. No text, no logos, no UI, no people, no paper, no strong glare.
